Book One: The Last Map of Cleopatra

Georgia has one enormous dream: to find Cleopatra's lost tomb. When she notices something strange in an old expedition map at a Sydney museum, the folds reveal a message no one has read in over a hundred years.

Following a forgotten archaeologist's clues takes Georgia towards the ruins west of Alexandria, and a discovery that may be only the beginning.

About the story

One map. A century-old secret. A new adventure.

A crown. A seven-pointed star. An ibis. Each clue Georgia discovers raises new questions about the work of archaeologist Evelyn Mercer, and about the people determined to follow the same trail.

The Last Map of Cleopatra blends historical curiosity, puzzle-solving and adventure for readers who love figuring things out for themselves.

The story begins with an old map, but some discoveries are too important to rush.
