**Status:** Work in progress, **no Beat 04 artwork approved**. Do not change the website's Dare placeholder yet. Beat 03 Follow remains locked.

## Meaning of Dare
Notice: a detail catches their attention.
Ask: they question it.
Follow: they choose to pursue a lead.
**Dare: they commit to a course of action despite uncertainty.**
Discover: something changes how they see the world.

This is not generic bravery or constant danger. It's the particular moment of **choosing to go forward**.

- Current draft: a cinematic, semi-realistic 16:9 standalone image of Georgia stepping onto a narrow weathered rope bridge across a lush forest gorge, an unusual tree-framed tunnel ahead.

## Grace & Byte — Dare, production brief (no usable art yet)
**Standalone 16:9 image only. No Georgia, no panels, no headings, no writing, no logos, no promotional poster, and no Survey-7.**

- Use the **approved Grace and Byte character sheets** and approved Sky City/Follow illustrations as identity references. Style: colourful, slightly stylised adventure animation matching their book-derived world; **not photoreal** and not a different cartoon style.
- Grace is approximately seven: youthful rounded face, freckles, big expressive brown eyes, short dense copper-red curls, grey technical explorer outfit with backpack and boots. Wrist computer on her LEFT wrist only.
- Byte is a **robot cat**: white/silver hard-shell armour with logical seams and articulated joints, black striped markings, large green eyes, glowing blue accents and a jointed, ringed tail, matching character sheet. Byte stays at her side as an equal protagonist.
- Environment: bright, functioning, optimistic future city, populated and well-maintained. Verdant terraces, sky gardens, flowing waterfalls, glass pedestrian networks, transportation and busy people in the background; not abandoned, collapsed, dystopian, or a deserted ruin.
- Visual concept: Grace makes a decisive step from an ordinary garden terrace onto an unfamiliar **narrow illuminated pedestrian bridge** that has just activated above a landscaped city canal. The bridge leads toward a small, intriguing elevated access platform. Byte is beside her, alert and ready. Her body action and face convey courage mixed with excitement, rather than alarm or panic. It is clearly a meaningful choice, not another hologram-inspection scene.
- Composition: low three-quarter angle, characters readable in left third and middle foreground, route extending rightward into a lively city. Full 16:9 scene, no baked typography. A deliberate echo of Georgia's bridge concept without copying visual style.
- No Survey-7, no orange propeller drone, no extra characters in foreground, no duplicate wrist displays, no cat fur replacing Byte's armour.
