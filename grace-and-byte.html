Grace & Byte

Book One: The Map in the Drone

In a city built over the remains of the old world, Grace loves exploring and sharing what she finds. Her loyal companion Byte is no ordinary cat — he's a robot with a mind of his own.

When they discover an old survey drone, its hidden secrets point the way towards an adventure neither of them expected.

About the story

A forgotten drone. A hidden map. One very curious explorer.

Grace has a knack for looking where no one else thinks to look. She, Byte and a mysteriously abandoned survey drone soon find themselves following evidence left behind from another time.

With mystery, technology, friendship and lots of curiosity, The Map in the Drone is the first chapter-book adventure in the Grace & Byte series.

More details, the final cover and purchasing links will be shared when the book is ready for release.
