# Cameron Grindle — Evergreen Homepage Hero (v0.3)

## Brand
**Different worlds. Same instinct.** The Five Beats (Notice / Ask / Follow / Dare / Discover) remain the organising idea. The hero introduces two series; it is not duplicated as the first Notice beat.

## Chosen images
- `georgia-home-hero.webp` — Georgia exploring ruins with her journal; character-inspired rather than a portrait, in realistic cinematic adventure art. The Georgia Adventures are globally extensible and not tied to Egypt.
- `grace-byte-home-hero.webp` — The approved **Grace and Byte Discover the Sky City** artwork. Bright, green, lived-in future; Grace and Byte are the series constants. Survey-7 is specific to current books, not evergreen hero branding.

Both are 1672 × 941 pixels, export to WebP and keep the webpage's text in HTML, not in the art.

## Responsive
- Desktop (1024px+): one-line tagline, equal-width side-by-side 16:9 hero art with 16px gutter.
- Tablet (768–1023px): stacked headline, side-by-side images.
- Mobile (below 768px): two-line tagline; full-width Georgia above Grace & Byte.
- No carousel hiding either story world.

## Approved palette and typography
Paper `#FAF7F2`, ink `#172F32`, copper `#B8673A`. Fraunces titles and Atkinson Hyperlegible body/UI.

## Deployment
The preview site already references the two WebP files by exact filename in repository root. The files must be uploaded to the `cameron-grindle-preview` repository `main` branch for the images to appear. Keep the live `cameron-grindle-website` `main` branch unchanged. Corresponding layout work is also in that repo's `five-beats-redesign` branch.

All five beat-specific illustrations, character cards and newsletter remain in-progress placeholders and are not part of this hero approval.
